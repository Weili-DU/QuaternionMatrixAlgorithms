By means of a complex representation of a commutative quaternion matrix, the singular value 
decomposition and the generalized inverse problems of a commutative quaternion matrix are 
studied, and the corresponding theorems and algorithms are given. In addition, based on the 
singular value decomposition and generalized inverse of a commutative quaternion matrix, the 
numerical experiments for solving the least squares problem and the color image watermarking 
problem are given. Numerical experiments illustrate the eﬀectiveness and reliability of the 
proposed algorithms.

At present, with the rapidly developing of the above-mentioned disciplines, a large number of papers have been published to

commutative quaternion matrix, and established the necessary and suﬃcient conditions for the existence of a solution and a solution 
formula. Additionally, Hikmah et al. [19]studied  Cramer’s rule and adjoint method for the commutative quaternion linear equations.

8. Conclusions
In this paper, by means of the complex representation of a commutative quaternion matrix, ﬁrstly we study the singular value 
decomposition of a commutative quaternion matrix and give a theorem and algorithm for the singular value decomposition, where 
the singular values diﬀer from the complex ﬁeld and the quaternion ring in that they are not nonzero real numbers, but new 
forms 𝑎 +𝑏j. Secondly, we study the generalized inverse problem, give the generalized inverse of a commutative quaternion and the 
generalized inverse of a commutative quaternion matrix, and give the corresponding theorems and algorithms.
Finally, as applications, we apply these algorithms for solving the CQLS problem and the color image watermarking problem of 
a commutative quaternion matrix. Numerical experiments show that the proposed algorithms are well. The properties of SVD and
